# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Ning Lin, Luxi Chen, Huaguan Chen, Jiacheng Cen, Chongxuan Li, Wenbing Huang, and Hao Sun for developing a practical and mathematically grounded framework for planar symmetric pattern generation.

The paper connects group symmetry, universal approximation, pattern synthesis, connectivity-aware optimization, and mechanics in a way that makes the research unusually amenable to claim-by-claim inspection. This repository is an independent reproduction and audit record; its scoped verdicts and limitations should not be read as author review or endorsement.

# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

This is an independently reconstructed symbolic derivation with a
machine-checked certificate, not a Lean/Coq kernel proof. It relies on standard
Stone-Weierstrass and Hironaka/free-module results under the paper’s stated
periodic crystallographic assumptions. The finite 17-group rank check is
catalogue corroboration only and is not presented as empirical proof of a
universal theorem.
